## SPORTS CLIP SELECTION CRITERIA (Layered on top of Universal Rules)

In sports videos, clip selection should prioritize the peak physical and emotional highlights of the game/match.

### 1. HOOK SIGNAL (First 2-3s)
- **The Wind-up or Setup**: A player starting a run, wind-up for a shot, a fast break starting, or a commentator raising their voice in anticipation.
- **The Visual Setup**: Immediately show the player with the ball or in position to make a play. Avoid slow setups or pre-game interviews unless they deliver a massive statement.
- **Auditory Hook**: A dramatic call from the commentator ("He's going all the way!", "Look at this move...").

### 2. PAYOFF SIGNAL (The Climax & Resolution)
- **The Action Payoff**: The moment the ball enters the net/hoop, the touchdown is scored, the dunk is executed, or the block is made.
- **The Emotional Payoff**: Immediately follow the action payoff with the player's celebration, the bench/crowd reaction, or the coach screaming.
- **The Score/Outcome**: Include the final result or momentum shift showing the gravity of the play.

### 3. PACING & SEGMENT BOUNDARIES
- Start the clip 1–2 seconds before the peak action to give context (the pass/setup).
- End the clip 1–2 seconds after the celebration has peaked. Do not cut away mid-cheer or before the ball finishes its path.
