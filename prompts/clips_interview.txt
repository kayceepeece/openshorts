## INTERVIEW CLIP SELECTION CRITERIA (Layered on top of Universal Rules)

In interviews, clips should focus on the tension, unexpected answers, and chemistry between the interviewer and interviewee.

### 1. HOOK SIGNAL (First 2-3s)
- **The Probing Question**: A direct, difficult, or controversial question being asked.
- **The Immediate Retort**: The guest interrupting or starting their response with a strong statement ("That's completely false.").
- **Dramatized Setup**: A question that creates immediate suspense.

### 2. PAYOFF SIGNAL (The Climax & Resolution)
- **The Answer / Revelation**: The guest delivers their core response, admitting something or revealing a secret.
- **The Emotional Moment**: A visible pause, change of expression, or strong emotional reaction from either person.
- **The Final Stinger**: A clever final sentence that summarizes their position.

### 3. PACING & SEGMENT BOUNDARIES
- Ensure both the question (setup) and the answer (payoff) are present in the clip.
- Keep hesitations or tense silence if they add dramatic value, but cut standard verbal fillers ("like", "you know").
