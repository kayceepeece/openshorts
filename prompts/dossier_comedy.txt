### How To Clip Compliance (evaluate EVERY candidate)
Each ranked clip must pass the three How To Clip rules. Record the result for every candidate.
  A) HOOK CHECK — the first 2–3 seconds must open with EITHER a bold statement OR a bold question.
     For comedy, an audible crowd laugh or a strong punchline opening can serve as the hook if it is bold
     and self-contained. REJECT or re-cut anything opening with small talk, filler, pleasantries, or
     mid-thought context. If the strongest bit has a weak opening, use hook transplanting: pull a line
     made later in the video to the front (even if repeated).
  B) POINT CHECK — the punchline / joke / beat must land within the first 10 seconds.
     Cut dead space, "um"/"uh", repetition, rambling, and thinking aloud. For comedy, note when a bit
     needs part of its setup to land; if so, keep the setup minimal and still make the joke land by
     the 10-second mark.
  C) COMPLETE THOUGHT — the clip must end on a finished beat (satisfied, not cut off).
     REJECT clips that stop mid-joke or end on an unresolved "but here's the thing…" with no punchline.
Reject any clip failing B or C. For a failed hook (A) with otherwise strong content, re-cut the start
(hook transplanting) instead of dropping the clip. Label each ranked clip with its compliance status,
e.g. "A✓ B✓ C✓ D3" or "A✗→transplant B✓ C✓ D2".
