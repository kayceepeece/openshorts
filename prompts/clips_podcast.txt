## PODCAST CLIP SELECTION CRITERIA (Layered on top of Universal Rules)

In podcast and talk-show content, clip selection should prioritize high-interest conversational hooks, hot takes, humor, and host/guest chemistry.

### 1. HOOK SIGNAL (First 2-3s)
- **The Bold Claim or Question**: The speaker starts with a provocative statement or a direct, engaging question.
- **The Confession or Admissions**: "I have never told anyone this before..." or "The truth about..."
- **Controversial Setup**: Host or guest pitching a highly opinionated topic.

### 2. PAYOFF SIGNAL (The Climax & Resolution)
- **The Hot Take / Punchline**: The main argument is delivered with conviction, or the punchline of a joke is spoken.
- **The Reaction Payoff**: Show the other hosts/guests reacting (laughter, shock, argument, silent disbelief).
- **The Conclusion**: The thought is fully resolved, leaving the listener with a clear understanding of the speaker's point.

### 3. PACING & SEGMENT BOUNDARIES
- Avoid starting mid-sentence unless the sentence itself is a clear hook.
- Cut filler words and pauses strictly. Ensure the setup leads to the payoff within 15 seconds.
- Let the final reaction (laugh, nod, agreement) breathe for 0.5s before ending.
