## GAMING CLIP SELECTION CRITERIA (Layered on top of Universal Rules)

In gaming videos, streams, and let's plays, clips should capture high-intensity moments, clutch plays, tragic failures, and streamer reactions.

### 1. HOOK SIGNAL (First 2-3s)
- **High Tension Setup**: The streamer entering a tense fight, low health alerts, or saying "There's no way we survive this."
- **The Outplay Start**: The start of a sequence of kills or a complex move.
- **Auditory Alert**: Loud in-game sounds or streamer gasp.

### 2. PAYOFF SIGNAL (The Climax & Resolution)
- **The Win/Kill or Fail**: The moment the boss is defeated, the triple kill is scored, or the player spectacularly dies.
- **The Streamer Reaction**: The streamer screaming, laughing, celebrating, or raging (throwing hands up, facepalming).
- **The Chat/Lobby Reaction**: Any funny teammate comment or in-game victory screen.

### 3. PACING & SEGMENT BOUNDARIES
- Pacing must be extremely fast. If there is a 5-second silence during a fight, start the clip closer to the climax.
- Keep the visual action clean. Let the reaction run fully before cutting.
